## 1. What we collect

### You give us

| Data | Where it lives |
|---|---|
| Email address and password | Supabase Auth (`auth.users`; passwords are hashed by Supabase, never stored by us) |
| Name, username, bio, avatar | `user_profiles` |
| Role — student, teacher or parent | `user_profiles.role` |
| Country, qualification, exam board, subjects | `user_profiles`, `student_subjects` |
| Study preferences, goals, theme | `user_profiles`, `study_goals` |

### You create by using AIDemic

| Data | Where it lives |
|---|---|
| Notes, flashcards and any source material you paste or upload | `flashcard_decks`, `flashcards`, notes tables |
| Answers to practice and exam questions, including free-text answers | `exam_practice_attempts`, `assignment_attempts`, `mock_test_answers` |
| "Blurts" — everything you recall from memory on a topic | blurt tables |
| Confidence ratings, mastery estimates and identified misconceptions | `student_subtopic_mastery`, `mastery_events`, `topic_confidence`, `misconceptions` |
| Study sessions, streaks, spaced-repetition schedule | `study_sessions`, `review_queue_items` |

### Collected automatically

Sign-in timestamps and session cookies; IP address, used transiently for
rate-limiting on authentication endpoints (`src/lib/ipRateLimit.ts`) and not
retained as a profile attribute.

We do **not** use advertising cookies, and we do not sell personal data or share
it with data brokers.

## 3. AI processing — read this part

Generating questions, marking answers, producing notes and podcasts, and
reviewing blurts all work by sending your content to a third-party AI provider.

**What is sent:** the question, your answer or blurt text, any source material you
supplied, and the relevant subject/topic context.
**What is not sent:** your name, email address, or account identifiers.

The provider is configurable (`src/lib/ai/config.ts`); the default is **OpenAI**
(`api.openai.com`), and **OpenRouter** is supported as an alternative. The provider
actually in use for this deployment is **[PROVIDER]**, processing in **[REGION]**,
under **[DPA REFERENCE]**, and **[does / does not]** retain submissions or train on
them.

> Do not paste anything into AIDemic that you would not want processed by that
> provider — including personal details about yourself or anyone else.

**AI output can be wrong.** Marks, feedback and predicted grades are indicative
only, generated by a language model, and are not an official assessment of any
kind. See the [terms](terms-of-service.md).

## 5. Who can see your data

- **You** — everything in your own account.
- **Your teacher** — if you join their class: your assignment work, marks and the
  topic-level analytics for that class. Not your private notes or flashcards.
- **A linked parent** — read-only, and only after **you accepted the link**. They
  see progress, streaks, assignment completion, weak topics and predicted grades.
  They can never write to your account, and you can revoke a link you or they
  created (a link created by a teacher needs teacher approval to revoke).
- **Us** — only where necessary to run or support the service.

Isolation is enforced by Postgres Row-Level Security on every table, not by the
application alone.

## 7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction,
portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Write to
**[privacy@yourdomain]**; we respond within one month.

You can complain to the **Information Commissioner's Office** (ico.org.uk,
0303 123 1113). In India, to the **Data Protection Board of India**.

If your account was created by a school, send rights requests to the school —
they are the controller — and we will assist them.

## 9. Security

Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest; Row-Level Security for per-user
isolation; passwords hashed by Supabase Auth; a Content-Security-Policy on the
authenticated app; rate limiting on authentication endpoints. No system is
perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise.

To report a vulnerability, email **[security@yourdomain]**.
